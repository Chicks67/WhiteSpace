              How it works

              What the agent does

              Six steps, in this order, on every run. Each one is shown on the page as it happens.

              1. Match the concept

                Your concept is matched to one of 25 cuisines the lease score was tested on. A cuisine we did not test stops with a plain message.

              2. Find storefronts

                Describe your plan and the agent picks neighbourhoods from census data, then searches vacant storefronts in several. Or search one area, or bring two to five addresses of your own.

              3. Rank on city records

                Each storefront is scored by how busy its block is and how much of the same cuisine is in and around it, from NYC inspection records.

              4. Write the brief, then check it

                A positioning brief for the top pick. Every business it names is traced to Qloo, to city records near the address, or marked unverified. Lines no source backs up are rewritten or removed.

              5. Partners and next steps

                The nearest business improvement district, independent neighbours for cross-promotion, and the permits and checks ahead, each with a source link.

              6. Answer follow-ups

                Ask anything about the shortlist. The agent answers with the same tools, and numbers that appear in no tool result are flagged.

              Sources

              What each source does

              Each source is used only for the job a test says it does well.

              City records

              Where to open

              NYC Health Department inspection records and the Storefront Registry of vacant storefronts. Qloo is not part of the lease score, because adding it changed nothing.

              Census and jobs data

              Who lives and works nearby

              Target customers come from the census. Only 2 of 7 Qloo audience tags matched it.

              City records

              Who the competitors are

              Qloo's own list held 37% of them, so the product never says "no competitor does this".

              Qloo place records

              When competitors close, and what reviewers mention

              City records have none of this. A person checked the closing times on Google Maps: 29 of 30 matched. Price levels matched for 17 of 27, under our bar, so they are not shown.

              Qloo affinity, not validated

              Where else a restaurant's fans go

              Shown as Qloo's list and labelled as not validated. It is never used to choose where to open.

              Scene explorer

              Where else do their fans go?

              Name up to three places your customers already love. Qloo returns the places their fans also go, and the music and brands that crowd follows. No model is involved.
